StaSIUN

  • [[Five indicators (transport, economy, access, urban, commercial), weighted with Entropy + AHP (CR = 0.0072).||Lima indikator (transportasi, ekonomi, akses, urban, komersial), dibobot dengan Entropy + AHP (CR = 0,0072).]]
